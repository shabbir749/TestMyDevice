About this test

Check every button and the scroll wheel on your mouse and see an approximate polling rate. A button that fails to highlight, or a wheel that jumps the wrong way, usually means wear or dirt.

How to use the mouseer test

  1. Click each button inside the box. Each one lights up when pressed.
  2. Scroll up and down and confirm the counter follows the direction.
  3. Move the mouse quickly in circles inside the box to read the polling rate.
  4. Repeat on a different surface or USB port if readings look wrong.

Frequently asked questions

What is mouse polling rate?

It is how many times per second the mouse reports its position. Common values are 125, 500 and 1000Hz.

Why is my polling rate lower than advertised?

Browsers may limit events to the screen refresh rate. Chrome and Edge usually give more accurate readings than other browsers.

Why does my mouse double click by itself?

Switch wear is the usual cause. Click slowly here and watch whether a button lights up more than expected, then consider repair or replacement.
