About this test

Refresh rate is how many times per second your screen redraws the image, measured in hertz (Hz). This test reads the real rate your browser draws at, shows the time of each frame, and animates three objects so you can see the difference between native, 60 and 30 FPS.

How to use the screen refresh rate (hz) test

  1. Keep this tab visible and close heavy apps so the reading is stable.
  2. Wait a few seconds until the Hz value settles.
  3. Compare the three moving objects. The top one runs at your full refresh rate and should look smoothest.
  4. If the value is lower than advertised, check your display settings, cable and power mode.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my 144Hz monitor show 60Hz?

Windows may still be set to 60Hz, or the cable or port cannot carry the higher rate. Open Display settings, Advanced display and pick the highest refresh rate.

What is a good frame time?

At 60Hz one frame takes about 16.7 ms, at 120Hz about 8.3 ms and at 144Hz about 6.9 ms. Big swings suggest stutter.

Does battery saver change the result?

Yes. Many laptops and phones lower the refresh rate to save power, so test while plugged in.
