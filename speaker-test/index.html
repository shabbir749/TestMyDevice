About this test

This tool plays clean test tones through the browser to check that both speakers or earbuds work and are not swapped. It also includes a 165 Hz tone that helps push small water droplets out of a phone speaker grille.

How to use the speaker & stereo test

  1. Set your volume low, then press Left channel. Only the left speaker or earbud should play.
  2. Press Right channel and confirm the sound moves to the right side.
  3. Press Stereo sweep and listen as the sound travels smoothly between sides.
  4. For water eject, face the speaker down, raise the volume and play the 165 Hz tone. It stops by itself after 30 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What if only one side plays?

Try other headphones or another app. If the problem stays, the speaker, cable or connector is likely damaged or blocked by dust.

Does the water eject sound really work?

Vibration can push small droplets out of the grille, but it cannot repair liquid damage inside. Let the device dry fully afterwards.

Can loud test tones damage my speakers?

Keep the volume moderate. Long loud low tones can stress small speakers, which is why the water eject tone stops after 30 seconds.
