How to use each tool

Dead pixel and screen test

Start the color test and cycle through solid colors. A dead pixel stays black on bright colors, a stuck pixel stays one color, and backlight bleed shows on black. Use the gradient test to spot color banding.

Refresh rate and FPS test

The counter shows your real refresh rate in Hz and the time of each frame. A 144 Hz monitor should report about 6.9 ms per frame. If you see 60 Hz, check your operating system display settings.

Microphone test

Allow access, speak normally and watch the bars and dB meter. Record five seconds and play it back to check clarity, background noise and volume.

Speaker and stereo test

Play the left and right channel tones to confirm each speaker works and is not swapped. The sweep moves sound between channels. The 165 Hz tone helps push water out of a phone speaker grille.

Keyboard and mouse test

Press every key to find dead keys, and hold several at once to check ghosting. For the mouse, test all buttons and the scroll wheel, then move it quickly to read the polling rate.

Webcam test

Start the camera to see the live image and resolution, such as 720p or 1080p. Use the snapshot button to save a frame and judge sharpness and color.

Tips for buying a used phone or laptop

  • Run the color test in a dim room to find dead pixels, burn-in and yellow or pink tints.
  • Check that the refresh rate matches the listing, especially on gaming laptops and 90/120 Hz phones.
  • Test both speakers and the microphone. Muffled sound can mean damage or water exposure.
  • On laptops, press every key and test the trackpad buttons and clicks.
  • Test the front camera. A blurry image may mean a scratched lens or a bad sensor.
  • Run all tests in the seller’s presence before paying, and check battery health in system settings.

Frequently asked questions

Is this device tester safe and private?

Yes. Every test runs locally in your browser. Audio, video and keystrokes are never uploaded or stored on a server.

Why does my browser ask for camera or microphone permission?

Browsers require your explicit permission before a web page can use the camera or microphone. Click Allow to run the test, and you can revoke it at any time in the site settings.

Why does my refresh rate show lower than my monitor specification?

The browser measures the rate it actually draws at. Battery saver mode, a wrong display setting, a background tab or a cable limit can lower it. Set the refresh rate in your operating system display settings and test again.

Does the 165Hz tone really remove water from a phone speaker?

The vibration of a low-frequency tone can push small water droplets out of the speaker grille. It will not fix liquid damage inside the device, so let the phone dry completely afterwards.

Can I use this tool to check a used phone or laptop before buying?

Yes. Open the page in the device browser and run the screen, audio, keyboard, touch and webcam tests during the meeting with the seller.
