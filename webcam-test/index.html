About this test

Test your laptop or USB webcam before a video call. The tool shows a live preview, detects the real resolution and frame rate, and lets you mirror the picture or save a snapshot to check sharpness and color.

How to use the webcam test

  1. Press Start camera and choose Allow in the browser prompt.
  2. Check the resolution under the preview. 1280×720 is HD and 1920×1080 is Full HD.
  3. Use Mirror to switch between a mirror view and the true view others see.
  4. Press Download snapshot to save a frame and judge focus and lighting.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my webcam showing a black screen?

Another app may be using the camera, a privacy shutter may be closed, or camera access is blocked for the browser in system settings.

Why is the resolution lower than my camera box says?

Browsers choose a mode based on lighting and USB bandwidth. Try a different USB port or better light.

Are my video or photos uploaded?

No. The preview and snapshots stay on your device.
