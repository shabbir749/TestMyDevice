About this test

Use this test before a meeting, stream or call to confirm your microphone works and sounds clear. It shows a live frequency graph, the current and peak level in dBFS, and lets you record five seconds and play them back in your browser.

How to use the microphone test

  1. Press Start microphone and click Allow when the browser asks.
  2. Speak normally and watch the bars move. Your loudest words should reach roughly -20 to -6 dBFS.
  3. Press Record & Playback 5s and listen for noise, echo or distortion.
  4. If nothing moves, check the selected input device in your system sound settings.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my microphone not working in the browser?

The site may be blocked in your browser permissions, another app may be using the mic, or the wrong input device is selected in system settings.

What does dBFS mean?

It measures digital level where 0 is the maximum. Values close to 0 can clip and distort, very low values sound quiet.

Is my voice uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording and playback happen only in your browser and the audio is discarded when you close the page.
